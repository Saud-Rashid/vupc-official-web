About VUPC

— VUPC

VUPC

The Varendra University Programming Club (VUPC) is a student-run community dedicated to competitive programming, problem solving, and building a culture of algorithmic thinking on campus.

We run regular practice sessions, bootcamps, and contests — Code Harbor is our flagship annual event.

— Department of CSE

Dept. of CSE

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Varendra University, provides academic and logistical support for VUPC's initiatives — from lab access to faculty mentorship.

Together, we aim to give every CSE student a real shot at competitive programming.
